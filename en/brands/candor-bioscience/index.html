About Candor Medix Biochemica

CANDOR Bioscience GmbH was founded in 2004 in Wangen im Allgäu, Germany, and manufactures in its own plant with standard batches ranging from 1 to 1,000 litres. The company is part of the Medix Biochemica group. The whole range addresses a single problem: making an immunoassay repeatable when the matrix, the background or conjugate storage make it unstable.

When a dedicated buffer is genuinely needed

The signal that points to it is nearly always the same: the curve works on the standard and falls apart on the real sample. In that case the problem is not the antibody, it is the matrix, and it is addressed with an interference reducer rather than by replacing the reagent. Typical cases are high background in serum, loss of conjugate activity in storage, and drift between batches of coated plates.

How CABRU frames the enquiry

CABRU starts from the observed problem — background, sensitivity, batch-to-batch reproducibility, stability over time — and from the assay format, and points to the reference on that basis. Where no catalogue product solves it, the manufacturer states that it develops analyte-specific solutions: the request should be sent with the assay data. Frequently asked questions

Who distributes Candor Medix Biochemica in Italy?

CABRU distributes Candor Medix Biochemica in Italy and handles technical enquiries, quotes and orders as the local point of contact.

What is LowCross-Buffer for?

It is an interference reducer, used when the real sample gives background or inconsistent signal compared with the standard, typically because of matrix interference or heterophilic antibodies.

Do the solutions only work with ELISA?

No. They are also designed for western blot, line blot, immunohistochemistry, immunoprecipitation, immunofluorescence, protein array and immuno-PCR.

Are animal-protein-free blocking agents available?

Yes: the range includes animal-protein-free variants as alternatives to BSA- or milk-based blockers.
