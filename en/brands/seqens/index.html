SEQENS in Italy

CABRU distributes SEQENS-branded BSA in Italy: bovine serum albumin fraction V, protease free, standard grade, in lyophilised form. The CABRU catalogue lists 3 references, corresponding to the 100 g, 500 g and 1 kg formats of the same grade.

About SEQENS

SEQENS is an international group active in specialty chemistry and life-science ingredients, with a bovine serum albumin line intended for laboratory use. On the Italian market CABRU handles only the BSA line of this brand: the group's other activities are not part of the representation.

What protease free fraction V BSA is

Fraction V refers to the historical serum fractionation method the albumin comes from: it is the designation under which the reagent entered laboratory practice and under which protocols still call for it. The protease free qualifier indicates the absence of residual protease activity, which in generic-grade BSA can degrade the analyte or the antibody during incubation. In long protocols that is the difference between a clean background and a signal that fades.

What it is used for in the laboratory

  • Blocking agent in ELISA, western blot and immunohistochemistry, to saturate non-specific binding sites on the solid phase. → ELISA kits · Electrophoresis and western blot
  • Stabiliser for antibodies, enzyme conjugates and dilute proteins, which in very dilute solution tend to adsorb onto container walls.
  • Reference protein for calibration curves in protein assays. → Proteins
  • Carrier in the preparation of solutions, standards and diluents. → Solutions and powders

The catalogue formats

The three references in the CABRU catalogue are the same product in three pack sizes: 100 g, 500 g and 1 kg. The choice is almost always a matter of annual consumption and storage rather than specification: lyophilised BSA keeps well, but a container opened and repeatedly sampled in a humid environment is the first cause of variability between runs.

When BSA is a critical reagent, not a consumable

Laboratories treat BSA as a consumable until a change of lot shifts the curve. At that point grade, fractionation method and lot-to-lot consistency start to matter. CABRU handles a BSA enquiry on those terms: which use, which grade the protocol calls for, and what continuity is needed over time.
