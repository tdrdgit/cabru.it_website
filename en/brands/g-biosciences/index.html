G Biosciences in Italy

CABRU distributes G Biosciences in Italy. G Biosciences is the brand of Geno Technology, Inc., a US manufacturer based in St. Louis (Missouri), active in kits and reagents for proteomics, molecular biology and general laboratory chemistry. CABRU s.a.s. carries the price list in Italy from its Arcore (MB) office, for technical information, quotes and orders: the G Biosciences catalogue managed by CABRU comprises 6,604 references, corresponding to 3,929 distinct products.

About G Biosciences

This is the brand that covers everything around the experiment: extracting, quantifying, separating, purifying, staining, buffering. Not a catalogue of analytes like those of the immunoassay manufacturers, but of consumables and process chemistry, with a range of pack sizes reaching industrial quantities. Hence the logic of the price list, ordered by technique rather than by biological target.

What the G Biosciences catalogue distributed by CABRU contains

The 6,604 references group by stage of work:

  • Proteomics and purification — 237 references across agaroses and chromatography resins, 235 across columns and devices, 44 for protein quantification, 32 digestion enzymes and 32 magnetic-bead products. → Electrophoresis & western blot
  • Electrophoresis and western blot — 107 references across stains and gel materials, 39 across membranes and transfer reagents, plus 111 reagents for labelling, biotinylation and cross-linking.
  • Buffers, solutions and powders — 372 references across buffers and ready solutions, 108 detergents and 81 antibiotics. → Solutions & powders
  • Molecular biology — 156 references across DNA and RNA extraction and handling and PCR reagents. → Nucleic acids
  • Microbiology — 62 references across culture media, broths and agars. → Microbiology culture media

Pack sizes: from micrograms to kilograms

There are 474 distinct pack sizes, and this is what sets the brand apart from the others represented: 3,202 references are expressed in milligrams, 1,021 in millilitres or litres, 828 in grams, 600 in reaction or preparation units and 291 in micrograms. Recurring sizes run from 5 mg on 930 references and 25 mg on 829, through to 500 ml on 119, one litre on 119, one kilogram on 44 and one gallon on 61. Laboratories that consume reagents by volume find the large size here, and on the same substances it changes the cost per gram appreciably.

Teaching kits

One section of the catalogue is devoted to teaching: 71 references are classified as student kits and cover practicals in affinity chromatography, bacterial transformation, conjugation, Gram staining, protein quantification by the Biuret or Coomassie method and genomic DNA extraction. 58 of them are sized for six groups of four or five people, that is 24 to 30 students per pack — a useful figure for anyone planning a teaching laboratory who has to quantify purchases before the course starts.

How to order G Biosciences in Italy
