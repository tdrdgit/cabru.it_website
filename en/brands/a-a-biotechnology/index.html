A&A Biotechnology in Italy

CABRU distributes A&A Biotechnology in Italy. The Polish manufacturer of molecular biology kits and reagents was founded in 1993 in Gdańsk, and 337 A&A references appear in the CABRU catalogue, covering nucleic acid extraction, purification and amplification.

About A&A Biotechnology

A&A Biotechnology is based in Gdańsk, Poland, and states that it operates its own research laboratory and production plant, manufacturing in house the enzymes used in its kits. Alongside manufacturing, the company runs service laboratories for DNA and RNA extraction, sequencing and microorganism identification. That structure lets it adapt formats and protocols, which is why several scale variants of the same kit appear in the catalogue.

What A&A Biotechnology makes

  • Extraction and purification kits — genomic DNA, plasmid DNA, total RNA and microRNA, high molecular weight DNA, starting from cultures, blood, tissues and environmental samples. → Nucleic acids
  • MagnifiQ platform — magnetic-bead kits for automatable extraction and NGS clean-up: 49 of the references in the CABRU catalogue belong to this line.
  • PCR and reverse transcription reagents — polymerases, ready-to-use mixes and cDNA synthesis reagents.
  • Molecular weight markers and electrophoresis reagents: 11 DNA markers in the catalogue. → Electrophoresis and western blot

Application areas

A&A references are used in genotyping, gene expression studies, sample preparation for Sanger sequencing and NGS, and molecular microbiology. The manufacturer names among its users university and research laboratories, veterinary laboratories, healthcare facilities and environmental and forensic laboratories: a range built around the variety of sample matrices rather than around a single protocol.

A&A Biotechnology references in the CABRU catalogue

The 337 A&A references in the CABRU catalogue all sit in the molecular biology area. The best represented families are genomic DNA kits (45 references), RNA and microRNA kits (46), the MagnifiQ magnetic-bead line (49) and plasmid DNA kits (15). Every entry carries code, name and pack size, so a search can start either from the code or from the kit name.

How CABRU handles A&A Biotechnology
