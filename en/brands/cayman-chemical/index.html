Cayman Chemical in Italy

CABRU distributes Cayman Chemical in Italy. Cayman Chemical is a US manufacturer of life-science research reagents, based in Ann Arbor (Michigan) and active since 1980, recognised for lipid and eicosanoid chemistry. CABRU s.a.s., based in Arcore (MB), handles technical enquiries, quotes and orders for the Italian market: the Cayman catalogue managed by CABRU comprises 67,119 references, corresponding to 23,429 distinct products offered in several pack sizes. It is the largest of the eighteen brands CABRU represents.

About Cayman Chemical

Cayman develops and manufactures small biochemical molecules, immunoassays and high-purity reagents in-house. Lipid-mediator synthesis is the company's historical field and shows in the catalogue distributed in Italy: 1,136 prostaglandin references, 167 leukotrienes, 114 sphingosines, 94 resolvins and 33 lipoxins. Cayman Chemical products are intended exclusively for research use and quality control. They may not be used in the formulation of pharmaceutical, galenic, cosmetic or food-supplement preparations, for human or animal use.
