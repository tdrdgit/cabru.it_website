ReliaTech in Italy

CABRU distributes ReliaTech in Italy. ReliaTech GmbH — the name stands for Receptor Ligand Technologies — is a German manufacturer based in Wolfenbüttel, Lower Saxony, specialised in recombinant proteins, cytokines, growth factors and antibodies for research. CABRU s.a.s. represents it from Italy, out of Arcore (MB), as a single point of contact for technical information, quotes and orders: the ReliaTech catalogue managed by CABRU comprises 3,444 references, corresponding to 2,138 distinct products.

About ReliaTech

ReliaTech manufactures its own proteins and antibodies, and adds to the catalogue a protein and antibody production and formulation service to customer specification. It is a small operation, vertical on one precise subject — the biology of receptors and their ligands — rather than a general catalogue: the depth lies in the families, not in the breadth of product types.

What the ReliaTech catalogue distributed by CABRU contains

The 3,444 references fall into three groups:

  • Antibodies — 1,798 references, polyclonal and monoclonal, for western blot, ELISA, flow cytometry and immunohistochemistry. → Antibodies
  • Recombinant proteins — 1,633 references across cytokines, growth factors and soluble receptors; 116 are available with a His-tag, 60 with an Fc-tag and 30 in biotinylated form. → Proteins
  • Specialities — 13 references covering culture supplements and extracts, such as the bovine and porcine ECGF material intended for blood and lymphatic endothelial cells.

Best-represented molecular families

Counting occurrences in product names, the most frequent families are the interleukins with 394 references, the EGF family with 252, VEGF with 208, FGF with 151, CCL chemokines with 142 and CXCL with 106, receptors with 116, TNF with 96 and PDGF with 29. Lymphatic endothelium markers also appear: 19 podoplanin references, 18 angiopoietins and 13 LYVE.

Declared applications and species

1,780 antibodies out of 1,798 carry the applications validated by the manufacturer: western blot on 1,467 references, immunohistochemistry on 390 across paraffin and frozen tissue, ELISA on 354, flow cytometry on 254, immunoprecipitation on 59 and immunofluorescence on 51. A further 93 capture pairs and 18 detection pairs are catalogued for building sandwich assays. Species coverage is mainly human with 2,282 references, mouse with 862 and rat with 168, plus bovine, chicken, porcine and zebrafish.

How to order ReliaTech in Italy

The route is short: the enquiry reaches CABRU, which checks it with the manufacturer in Germany, issues the quote and oversees shipping to the laboratory, in Italian or English. There are 17 pack sizes in all, from the 2 µg trial amounts to the 6 mg process lots: the 100 µg size alone covers 1,686 references.

The same protein often exists in several variants — tagged, untagged, biotinylated, in different sizes — and the wrong choice tends to surface once the assay is already set up.
