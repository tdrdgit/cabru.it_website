LDN (Labor Diagnostika Nord) in Italy

CABRU distributes LDN — Labor Diagnostika Nord in Italy, a German manufacturer founded in 1996 in Nordhorn, specialising in immunoenzymatic kits for biogenic amines, neurotransmitters and hormones. The CABRU catalogue lists 102 references, 97 of them in ELISA format.

About LDN

LDN was founded in 1996 in Nordhorn, Germany, with the aim of developing and manufacturing specialty in vitro test systems. The determination of biogenic amines by immunological methods has remained the core of its work. Alongside its catalogue kits, the company states contract development and manufacturing activity, and takes part in external quality assessment schemes such as INSTAND, RfB, ARVECON and FAPAS.

What LDN makes

  • Biogenic amines — catecholamines, plasma and urine metanephrines, serotonin, histamine: the company's founding area. → ELISA kits
  • Neuroendocrine markers — chromogranin A and 5-HIAA, alongside neurotransmitters and amino acids.
  • Endocrinology — testosterone and free testosterone, DHEA, 17-OH-progesterone, cortisol, melatonin, AMH, with saliva-specific versions.
  • Food control — kits and control materials for histamine in food, including fish controls. → Biochemicals

LDN references in the CABRU catalogue

Of the 102 LDN references in the CABRU catalogue, 97 are ELISA kits. Fourteen belong to the Fast Track line, which shortens run time compared with the extended protocol for the same analyte. Several references exist in more than one version depending on the matrix — plasma, urine, saliva — and for histamine the catalogue also includes control materials for the food matrix.

Application areas

Research and routine laboratory work in endocrinology, neuroscience and neuroendocrine biomarkers; stress and circadian rhythm studies through salivary cortisol and melatonin; food safety and intolerance, particularly for histamine. These are kits chosen from the analyte and the matrix, not from the instrument platform.

Analyte, matrix, protocol: the three questions

LDN references look alike by name and differ on three points: which analyte, on which matrix, and with which protocol length. A metanephrine assay on plasma is not the same as on urine, and the Fast Track version is not the same kit as the extended one. CABRU asks for those three details before quoting, which is why the quote comes back with the correct code first time. Frequently asked questions

Who distributes LDN in Italy?

CABRU distributes LDN (Labor Diagnostika Nord) in Italy and handles technical enquiries, quotes and orders as the local point of contact.

What kind of ELISA kits does LDN offer?

Kits for biogenic amines (catecholamines, metanephrines, serotonin, histamine), neuroendocrine markers such as chromogranin A and 5-HIAA, and steroid hormones. The CABRU catalogue lists 97 ELISA references out of 102 in total.

What distinguishes the Fast Track line?

It is a shortened-protocol version for the same analyte, with reduced run time compared with the extended format. The CABRU catalogue lists 14 Fast Track references.

Are saliva kits available?

Yes: several analytes exist in saliva-specific versions, including cortisol, melatonin, DHEA and 17-OH-progesterone.

Does LDN also cover histamine control in food?

Yes: the catalogue includes kits and control materials for histamine in food matrices, including fish controls.

Is CABRU the exclusive distributor of LDN?

No. CABRU distributes LDN in Italy on a non-exclusive basis.
