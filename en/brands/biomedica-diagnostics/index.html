BioMedica Diagnostics in Italy

CABRU distributes BioMedica Diagnostics in Italy, a Canadian manufacturer specialising in haemostasis and thrombosis, based and manufacturing in Windsor, Nova Scotia. The range covers chromogenic and fluorogenic substrates, controls and calibrators, antibodies, proteins and coagulation panels.

About BioMedica Diagnostics

BioMedica Diagnostics is based in Windsor, Nova Scotia (Canada), where it runs a facility of roughly 4,550 square metres with automated manufacturing, dispensing and freeze-drying. The company names thrombosis and haemostasis as its core competencies and is part of the Scotia Investments group. It also states contract manufacturing capability for OEM, bulk and intermediary materials, along with custom labelling.

What BioMedica Diagnostics makes

  • Chromogenic and fluorogenic substrates for measuring enzyme activity in the coagulation cascade. → Biochemicals
  • Antibodies and proteins against coagulation factors and thrombotic risk markers. → Antibodies · Proteins
  • Controls and calibrators, and plasmas for assay calibration and quality control. → Plasmas
  • Panels for specific clinical areas — anticoagulant therapy, antiphospholipid syndrome, bleeding profiles, D-dimer, thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura, von Willebrand disease, cancer prognosis. → ELISA kits

Application areas

Haemostasis and thrombosis research and development, calibration and quality control of coagulation assays, assessment of anticoagulant therapy and study of thrombotic risk markers. The manufacturer names human and animal health, research, education and in vitro diagnostics as its fields. → Haemostasis and coagulation
