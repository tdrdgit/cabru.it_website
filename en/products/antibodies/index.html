Antibodies for research

What this category covers

Primary antibodies (monoclonal, polyclonal, clonal) and conjugated secondary antibodies, usable across a wide range of applications: western blot (WB), ELISA, immunofluorescence (IF), immunocytochemistry (ICC), immunohistochemistry (IHC), immunoprecipitation (IP), flow cytometry (FC), ChIP.

Who makes them

CABRU supplies antibodies from several manufacturers: FineTest, Cayman Chemical, ExBio, ReliaTech, BioVendor, IMMBIOMED, Affinity Biologicals and BioMedica Diagnostics.

How to request technical-scientific information and/or a quotation

To receive technical-scientific information and/or a quotation, simply provide either the code of the selected antibody or the product name or, more generally, the antigen and reactivity, and send an e-mail to info@cabru.it.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a primary and a secondary antibody?

The primary antibody binds the target antigen directly; the secondary binds the primary and carries a detection label (enzyme or fluorophore). Further detail is available in the dedicated guide.

Does CABRU supply antibodies for flow cytometry?

Yes, in particular ExBio's conjugated monoclonals. Details are given on the ExBio page.

Is it possible to request an antibody not listed in the catalogue?

Yes: from the target description, CABRU verifies availability among the manufacturers it represents.
