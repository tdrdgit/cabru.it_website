Quality & certifications

The quality of the products that reach your lab is built twice: by CABRU's certified ISO 9001 quality management system, and by the certified quality systems of the manufacturers we select and represent in Italy.

CABRU's quality system

CABRU s.a.s. operates a quality management system that conforms to the ISO 9001:2015 standard. The system governs supplier selection and qualification, order management, product storage and delivery, and the handling of non-conformities and complaints, and is subject to periodic surveillance audits by the certification body.

What it means for your lab

  • Products that come from manufacturers with certified quality systems, selected also for their reliability.
  • Batch traceability and technical documentation (e.g. certificates of analysis) available on request.
  • Storage and transport compliant with the manufacturer's instructions, cold chain included where required.
  • A single point of contact overseeing the whole process, from enquiry to delivery, under CABRU's quality system.
  • Structured handling of orders, non-conformities and complaints.
