How to request a quotation

There are no public prices on this website: every supply is quoted against the specific request, because the price depends on the format, the quantity and the manufacturer's conditions at the time of the order. To receive a quotation it is enough to state the code or the name of the product and the quantity required.

  • From the online catalogue: search for the product, add it to the quotation request and send the whole list in one step.
  • By email to info@cabru.it, or through the contact form reachable from the “Contact us” button on every page.
  • By phone on +39 039 6013988, if a technical discussion about the right product comes first.

CABRU checks availability, format and conditions with the represented manufacturer and sends the quotation, with a single point of contact for the whole request. Indicative response time: to be completed — e.g. “within 2 working days”.
